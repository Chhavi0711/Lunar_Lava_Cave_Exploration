# References

This project's literature review and structural modeling approach draw on the following peer-reviewed sources:

1. X. Zhou, S. Zhou, D. Li, D. Cui, C. Dong, "Research on design and cushioning performance of combined lunar landing airbag," *Acta Astronautica*, vol. 191, pp. 55–78, 2022. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actaastro.2021.10.033

2. M. Alizadeh, A. Sedaghat, E. Kargar, "Shape and Orifice Optimization of Airbag Systems for UAV Parachute Landing," *International Journal of Aeronautical & Space Sciences*, vol. 15, no. 3, pp. 335–343, 2014. https://doi.org/10.5139/IJASS.2014.15.3.335

3. X. Zhou, S. M. Zhou, D. K. Li, "Optimal Design of Airbag Landing System without Rebound," *IOP Conference Series: Materials Science and Engineering*, vol. 531, 012001, 2019. https://doi.org/10.1088/1757-899X/531/1/012001

4. J. Wen, B. Li, Z. Yang, "Study on cushioning characteristics of soft landing airbag with elastic fabric," *International Journal of Applied Electromagnetics and Mechanics*, vol. 33, pp. 1535–1545, 2010. https://doi.org/10.3233/JAE-2010-1282
